01 / Background

From fire science to your spreadsheets.

I'm a data scientist. For the past six years I've worked in wildfire science with the US Forest Service and a wildfire-risk startup, building data pipelines, fire spread models and the Python and GIS tooling around them.

Most of the people I built for were experts in fire, not in code. That taught me the part that matters most: a tool only counts if the people it's for can run it without me. Time on prescribed burn crews kept me honest about how plans hold up in the field.

Along the way I started building Claude skills so my team could rerun our own workflows without me in the loop. That system became Skillification, and it's the core of what I offer now.

01 / What's a skill?

A recipe Claude follows the same way every time.

A skill is a short set of instructions, plus any templates or scripts, that tells Claude how your team does a specific task. Which files to look at, what steps to follow, what the output should look like.

Once it's saved, anyone on the team can run it by name. No prompt writing, no copying and pasting last month's version, no "it worked when Sarah did it."

02 / Examples

The boring, repeatable work is exactly what skills are for.

/weekly-report

Pulls the numbers from your spreadsheet and writes the Monday update in your format.

/client-intake

Turns a new inquiry email into a project folder, a checklist, and a draft reply.

/quote-builder

Builds a quote from your price sheet and past jobs, ready for you to review.

/invoice-check

Compares invoices against timesheets and flags anything that doesn't line up.

/meeting-to-tasks

Turns meeting notes into assigned tasks with owners and due dates.

/data-cleanup

Takes the messy export from your other software and makes it usable.

03 / How it works

Built with your people, not handed to them.

Skills nobody asked for don't get used. Every skill starts from a real task someone on your team already does.

01 Audit

Map the work

A short interview with each person. I write up their slice of the workflow and flag what Claude could take on first.

02 Build

Do one real task

We do one of their actual tasks with Claude, side by side. If the result is good, it gets saved as their skill.

03 Share

One team set

I merge everyone's skills into one standard library. Personal tweaks stay as small add-ons so nobody loses their version.

04 Maintain

Keep it working

Skills ask for quick feedback after each run. I use that, plus Claude's updates, to keep the set sharp.

06 / Questions

Fair questions.

Does my team need to know how to code?
No. Your team uses skills by talking to Claude in plain English. The behind-the-scenes setup is my job, and the guide I leave you is written for non-technical people.
Which Claude plan do we need?
Each person needs a paid Claude account that supports skills. The right plan depends on your team size and how you share files, so we'll pick it together on the first call before you buy anything.
What happens to our files and data?
Your files stay where they are, on your computers and in your own accounts. Skills contain instructions for how to do a task, not your business data.
What if Claude changes and something breaks?
The sprint includes 30 days of fixes. After that, Skill Upkeep covers it. If you'd rather handle it yourselves, the guide shows you how.
We're not sure AI is for us yet.
That's a fine place to start. The audit alone usually turns up a few tasks worth automating, with or without AI. If Claude isn't the right fit, I'll tell you, and my automation work might be.

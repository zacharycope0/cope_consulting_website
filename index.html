01 / The problem

Most teams try AI once, get a generic answer, and go back to doing it by hand.

The fix isn't a cleverer prompt. It's writing down how your team does the work, once, in a form Claude can follow every time. That's a skill. Your weekly report, your quote builder, your client intake. Same steps, same format, every run.

02 / Skillification

Four steps from "we should use AI" to a team that does.

01 Audit

Map the work

I sit down with each person and write up what they actually do all week, and what Claude could take off their plate.

02 Build

Do one real task

We do a real piece of their work together with Claude. If they like the result, we save it as a skill they can rerun.

03 Share

One team set

The best skills become one standard set the whole team shares, while each person keeps their own tweaks.

04 Maintain

Keep it working

Claude changes every few months. I keep your skills running, fold in feedback, and add new ones as your work changes.
